What is Taraka?

Taraka is a private, multi-currency personal finance app for Android. Track wallets, transactions, budgets, goals, and recurring bills — stored on your device by default, with optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

  • Multi-currency wallets (NTD, USD, IDR) with cross-currency net worth
  • Budgets with real-time progress and monthly reset
  • Goals with visual progress ring and goal-completion celebration
  • Recurring transactions (daily / weekly / monthly / yearly)
  • Analytics: net worth trend, category breakdown, custom date ranges
  • Quick Add notification for one-tap logging from the notification shade
  • Trilingual: English, Bahasa Indonesia, 繁體中文
  • Biometric lock, JSON / Excel export, dark and light themes
